about

Artist. Producer. Composer. Builder.

Born in São Paulo, I started making music at 9 and moved to Miami at 14. I've been part of Miami's music scene since the early 2000s, collaborating with musicians, artists and filmmakers.

My background crosses performance, audio technology, production and web development, and over time those disciplines stopped feeling separate. Today I'm interested in what happens when they collide:

when a drum machine behaves like a musician,
when physical data becomes a melody,
when software protects a forgotten idea,
when electronic music becomes genuinely live.

How can technology make the human part of the work more expressive?
