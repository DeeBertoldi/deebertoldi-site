Music for picture, movement and story.

For more than 15 years I've composed, produced and designed sound for film, commercials, theatre, dance and visual media.

Through my Miami studio Sounds For Visuals, founded in 2008, I create original scores and sound that support the emotional architecture of a story without disappearing into the background, from intimate composition and atmospheric sound design to electronic, rhythm-driven scores.
