Data Sonification

Experiments transforming information from the physical world into sound. Sensor data travels through Arduino and OSC into Ableton Live and becomes rhythm, pitch, modulation and musical movement in real time.

The environment becomes part of the composition.

sensors→Arduino→OSC→Ableton

performance

Live Performance Systems

Custom MIDI and hardware systems for electronic performance, connecting synthesizers, loopers, controllers and Ableton into one synchronized setup while keeping each machine free enough to play.

The goal isn't automation. It's a system that behaves more like a band.
