01 / producer

Records with their own identity.

For more than 15 years, I've worked with artists to develop music from the first idea through arrangement, production and final mix.

My approach is collaborative and detail-driven: finding the sonic language already hiding inside a song and pushing it somewhere distinctive. My work moves across electronic, Brazilian, alternative, experimental and cinematic music, drawing on keyboards, percussion, analog-electronic gear, sound design and mixing.

I work in English, Portuguese and Spanish.
