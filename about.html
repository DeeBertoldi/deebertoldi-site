About Daniella Bertoldi

Music producer, composer, sound designer and live electronic artist based in Miami.

biography

Daniella Bertoldi is a Miami-based music producer, composer, sound designer and live electronic artist whose work moves between electronic music, experimental composition, live performance, film and theater.

Born in São Paulo, she started learning music at 9 and moved to Miami at 14. Over a career spanning two decades, she has built a multidisciplinary practice rooted in curiosity, collaboration and a deep fascination with sound. Her musical language draws on Brazilian rhythms, alternative rock, trip-hop, techno, experimental electronics and atmospheric sound design, often bringing together organic instrumentation, synthesizers and unconventional sonic textures.

As a performing musician, Daniella has played in numerous bands and collaborative projects across more than 100 live events in the United States and abroad. She toured as keyboardist for Latin Grammy-nominated artist Mr. Pauer, performing in Los Angeles, New York, Bogotá and Medellín, and has collaborated with independent musicians, electronic artists and experimental performance ensembles.

Beyond the stage, she composes original music and designs sound for independent films, documentaries, theater and audiovisual projects. Her longstanding collaboration with Miami's Bistoury Physical Theatre and Film includes a 60-minute experimental score for TRIBE and music for the film Talashimaii.

Through her studio Sounds For Visuals, founded in 2008, she has contributed music, sound, recording and audio post-production to projects with brands and production companies including Honda, Nickelodeon, Google Sandbox, mitú Networks and Little Napoleon Productions.

Her live-event work extends beyond performing, into music supervision, sound production, artist coordination and event logistics for iiiPoints activations, Superfine! Art Fair, Ladyfest Miami, Miami Light Project's BOMBILLO and eMerge Americas.

Today she performs as Dee Bertoldi, a live electronic artist combining Ableton, loopers, synthesizers and MIDI hardware into evolving performances rooted in hypnotic rhythm and experimental sound.

At the heart of her practice is a belief that music is both an emotional language and a space for exploration, where composition, performance and technology continually inform one another.
