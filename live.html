02 / live

Electronic music performed as a living system.

Hypnotic techno at the core, with Brazilian rhythms and drum and bass woven through.

My live sets are built from Ableton, loopers like the Boss RC-505, synthesizers and MIDI hardware, and the setup adapts to the room. Part live band, part electronic set. Rather than simply playing finished tracks, I build structures that can change in the moment: rhythms, synth lines, textures and arrangements responding to one another in real time.
